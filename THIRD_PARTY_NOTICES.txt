Third-party data and code used by Typescript (crates/core/data)

Typescript is a Rust port of Seamtype and ships these word lists inside the DLL.

1. Seamtype (code and design)
   Copyright (c) 2026 aimdevlee. MIT License.
   https://github.com/aimdevlee/seamtype
   The MIT notice is reproduced in NOTICE (the project itself is AGPL-3.0, see LICENSE).

2. Korean vocabulary list (korean_vocabulary.txt) and English word frequencies
   (english_frequency.txt, the 50k list)
   Derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords, CC BY-SA 4.0.
   https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
   These files are NOT covered by the AGPL-3.0 license of this project; they stay under CC BY-SA 4.0
   (a modified copy must be shared under the same license).

3. Korean sentence-derived syllable and word frequencies (korean_syllables.txt, korean_words.txt)
   Derived from Tatoeba sentences, CC BY 2.0 FR. https://tatoeba.org
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
   korean_words.txt also holds one-syllable words that informal Korean says (tools/colloquial_words.py),
   found in the test half of the Naver sentiment movie corpus (NSMC), CC0. https://github.com/e9t/nsmc

4. English words and how common they are (english_words.txt)
   SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), http://wordlist.aspell.net
   Permissive license; see http://wordlist.aspell.net/scowl-readme/ for the full notice.

5. Letter-trigram training list (web2.txt)
   Webster's Second International Dictionary word list (/usr/share/dict/web2), public domain.

6. Kana trigram model (kana_trigrams.bin, the Japanese detector)
   Derived from Tatoeba sentences, CC BY 2.0 FR. https://tatoeba.org
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

7. IPADIC-derived data within the Japanese dictionary (see Mozc below)
   Copyright 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003 Nara Institute of Science and Technology.
   All Rights Reserved. Use, reproduction, and distribution of this data is permitted;
   any copy, in its original form or modified, must include this notice.
   https://github.com/taku910/mecab

8. Legacy IPADIC builder's optional word-frequency adjustments (tools/build_ipadic.py;
   not used in the current Mozc-derived ipadic_compact.bin)
   Made with wordfreq 3.1.1 (code Apache-2.0, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq);
   wordfreq's combined data is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0.
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

9. Mozc OSS dictionary and connection costs (ipadic_compact.bin, TSIPDIC4)
   https://github.com/google/mozc/tree/0136c35782536cb43d710575c311cd4185982738
   Google-authored components: BSD-3-Clause. Dictionary includes IPADIC (NAIST/ICOT)
   and Okinawa dictionary (public domain); preserve their separate notices.
   Full notices: crates/core/data/mozc/LICENSE.txt and DICTIONARY-LICENSE.txt,
   also distributed in the Japanese pack as mozc-LICENSE.txt and
   mozc-DICTIONARY-LICENSE.txt. Source hashes, revision and transformations:
   crates/core/data/ipadic_compact.sources.json, tools/build_mozc.py.
   Modified by Typescript: kana-only readings, exact-tuple deduplication,
   lossless packed representations and transposed connection matrix.
   No evaluation corpus is used to construct this dictionary.

10. Lab-only Japanese evaluation data (downloaded, not packaged or redistributed)
    AJIMEE-Bench JWTD_v2/v1: azooKey, CC-BY-SA 3.0, derived from Japanese Wikipedia
    input typo dataset v2 (Kyoto University) test data and Wikipedia.
    https://github.com/azooKey/AJIMEE-Bench/tree/401666cd56d1a570c2021798b64b6da4396bfd45
    License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
    Input script is normalized from katakana to hiragana; allowed answers are unchanged.
    Mozc OSS regression tests: google/mozc, BSD-3-Clause (same revision as above).
    Sources and SHA-256 hashes: lab/evaluate_japanese.py; cached in ignored lab/data/.
